      Pets

      Pets start at Level 1 and level twice as fast as crawlers until Level 15, then at the same rate, with 3 stat points per level (never Intelligence). Their attack/spell ranks equal the current Floor — pets do not have trackable skill ranks, so skills here are names only and show Rank = Floor. Health Bar slots start equal to size and gain 1 per level, to a maximum of 10. Tank pets get DR equal to the Floor and one Special Ability; Utility pets get two; Aggressive pets get none (they get Back Claw and Bite instead). At Level 15 a pet gains one more benefit, which goes in as a custom ability.

      Mounts

      Mounts do not level up like pets. They move you at no Action cost, get one Action per combat round, and do not attack unless they have Trample (Rank = Floor, Xd6 Bludgeoning where X is size). Mounting or dismounting costs a Move Action. Health Bar slots equal size. Base price is 20,000 gold for a size Large (5) mount with Move 30ft and Step 10ft; each upgrade below costs an extra 5,000 gold, and non-attack upgrades may be taken multiple times.

      Sponsors

      Banner Count is manual: Send Sponsor Banner adds one, and the −/+ buttons correct it. Nothing here is automatic.

      Faith

      Worship tiers: Acolyte, Devotee (highest for non-Clerics/non-Paladins), Zealot (Clerics/Paladins). Required tithe: 5% at Acolyte, 10% at Devotee, 20% at Zealot, of all looted gold. Every 5 consecutive days of worship (tithes and daily offerings) earns a Boon. Miss a day and you lose worship benefits until you offer the next day; miss two days and you gain a Divine Debuff; miss three and you gain Fallen from Grace (you cannot worship any deities, 50% chance of being smote). Church quests you must complete go on the Quests tab. In-game time is not real time, so streak and missed days are tracked by hand with the buttons below.

      Crafting

      Tables run tier 1–10. A brand-new tier-1 table costs 25,000 gold and levels up by 1 when you reach a new floor; upgrades come from Table Upgrade Coupons in loot boxes or cost 250,000 gold. A table's tier is a bonus to your Skill Check, and if you need multiple tables only the lowest-tier bonus applies. You cannot enchant an item with the exact same property twice, and enchanting gets risky after three enchantments. A "+1 Longsword" is really two enchantments: +1 Longsword Skill Rank and +1 damage bonus, crafted and rolled separately.

      Loot Boxes

      Boxes sent by your DM arrive here. There is no button to open just one box: Open all boxes opens every waiting box in a popup, one at a time, lowest grade first: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary, Celestial. Opening a box reveals its message and items, plays its sound if one was chosen, and adds its items to your Inventory. When all boxes are opened, the popup closes and the Loot Boxes tab is cleared.
